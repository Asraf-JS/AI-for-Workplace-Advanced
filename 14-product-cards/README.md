# 14 - Product Cards

Every lab in this course is written as a tool-neutral task. These cards show how to do the common steps (start a chat, upload a file, build a custom assistant, ground it in your files, check its sources) in the AI tool your organisation gives you.

> **Note:** each module's **Product Notes** table covers what's different for that lab. Use these cards for the general how-to.
