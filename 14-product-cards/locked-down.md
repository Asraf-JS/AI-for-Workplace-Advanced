# If Your Company Blocks a Tool

Many companies block consumer AI tools, or won't let a new service connect to the company mailbox. That's sensible: these are outside your company's systems. This page shows how to do every lab anyway.

> **Important:** whatever you use, upload only the course's fictional files. Never put real company, supplier or personal data into a tool your organisation hasn't approved.

---

## Ask IT Before Class

Three questions save time on the day:

1. Which AI tool are we approved to use for work data? (Copilot with the green shield, a ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini business plan, or none.)
2. Can I open Gemini Notebook (notebooklm.google) with a personal Google account on my work laptop?
3. Can I sign up for an n8n trial, and connect it to my work email? If not, may I use a personal email account for the training emails?

If the answer to 2 or 3 is no, use the alternatives below.

---

## Modules 04 and 09: No Gemini Notebook

Use a notebook in the tool you're approved for. The screenshots show Gemini Notebook, but the steps are the same idea: add the policy files as sources, ask the questions, check the citations.

| Tool | Where to start |
|---|---|
| Copilot (Basic or Premium) | In the Copilot app, select **Notebooks**, create a notebook, then **Add references** > **Upload files**. |
| ChatGPT | Create a **Project** and add the files to it. |
| Claude | Create a **Project** and add the files to its knowledge. |
| Gemini (Workspace) | Gemini Notebook may be allowed on your work account even if personal accounts are blocked. Ask IT. |

The differences: some tools show a citation as a link to the file rather than to the exact sentence. That's fine for these labs: ask for the clause and the exact sentence in your prompt (the 4.2 tip), and check it in the PDF yourself.

## Module 10: No n8n, or No Mailbox Connection

Pick whichever fits:

- **n8n is allowed, but not your work mailbox.** Connect a personal Gmail or Outlook.com account instead, and send the training emails only to that account. The data is fictional, so this is safe.
- **n8n isn't allowed at all.** Follow the trainer's build on screen. Before each of the four quotations runs in 10.7, write down the route you expect (approve, revise, reject or escalate) and why. Then compare with what the workflow does. You'll still use the design in Module 13.

Either way, the idea to take back is the same: the AI judges, a rule checks the facts, and a person approves.

## Where Your Data Goes

| Tool | Where the text you upload goes |
|---|---|
| Copilot with the green shield | Stays inside your organisation's Microsoft 365, under its enterprise data protection. |
| ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini business plans set up by your IT team | Covered by your organisation's agreement with the provider. Ask IT what it allows. |
| Free or personal plans, and Gemini Notebook on a personal account | Go to the provider under its consumer terms. Use only the fictional course files. |
| n8n trial | Runs on n8n's cloud. The AI step sends the quotation text to the AI model the trial uses. Use only the fictional course files. |

> **Tip:** this is exactly the question Module 12 asks you to answer for every AI use in your department.
