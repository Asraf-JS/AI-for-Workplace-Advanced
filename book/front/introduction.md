## How to use this book

This book collects the notes and prompts from the two-day AI for Workplace (Advanced) course in one place, so you can follow along offline and keep it at your desk afterwards.

Each chapter matches one module of the course and has two parts:

- **Notes** explain each topic and walk you through the lab, with screenshots, checkpoints and troubleshooting.
- **Hands-on exercises** give you every prompt you type, ready to copy.

Every lab is written as a tool-neutral task. Each module's **Product Notes** table says what's different in Copilot Chat (Basic), M365 Copilot (Premium), ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, and the Product Cards at the end cover the common steps in each tool.

Look out for the coloured boxes. Blue boxes are notes, teal boxes are tips, purple boxes are things to try, orange boxes are key points, and red boxes are warnings.

Prefer to copy prompts rather than retype them? Everything in this book is also online at **github.com/Asraf-JS/AI-for-Workplace-Advanced**, where each grey box has a copy button. Copying from there is safer than copying from a PDF, which can add line breaks in the middle of a prompt.

## Course scenario

This is the continuation of **AI for Workplace (Beginner)**. You build one assistant on Day 1 and keep improving it: grounding it in company policy, producing on-brand documents from templates, measuring its accuracy, and on Day 2 rebuilding it as an agent workflow with human approval.

All lab data is for the fictional company Sinar Maju Sdn Bhd.

## Ground rules

> **Important:** use only the fictional lab data during class. Please do not upload real company, customer or personal data into any AI tool unless your organisation has approved that tool for it. AI can be wrong: check its numbers and claims before you use them.
